# Triage runbook

```console
python -m cloud_security_lab detect fixtures/events/demo.json --format json
```

Output includes source event IDs, principal, timestamp, related evidence, reason, disposition, and investigation steps. Individual alerts are `REVIEW`; the documented policy-change → logging-disable sequence is `ESCALATE`. Neither means a confirmed incident.

## Review an alert

1. Confirm event ID, timestamp, recipient account, and exact principal/session ARN. Inspect `eventSource`, outcome, and parameters.
2. Read the [predicate](../detections/cloud-detections.md). A policy-version event does not by itself prove privilege expansion.
3. Identify missing context: change ticket, business owner, previous policy, authentication history, or network justification. The lab does not fabricate these.
4. Record what is known and what needs investigation. An approved action may be benign; that decision requires human evidence.

For no-MFA alerts, the supported case is a successful IAM-user login explicitly reporting `MFAUsed: No`. Do not infer federated MFA posture from this signal. Break-glass exceptions require an owner and documented review.

## Review a sequence

`COR-001` joins a successful IAM policy-version change to a later successful `StopLogging` or `DeleteTrail` for the same account and exact principal/session ARN within 15 minutes, inclusive. Simultaneous or reverse-ordered events do not qualify.

Inspect both event IDs and elapsed time. Identify the policy and trail from available parameters. This sequence warrants priority review, but authorized maintenance can also match it. The lab does not infer that new privileges were used.

## Close-out

Use [configuration verification](remediation-checklist.md) for posture findings. Historical alerts remain observations after a fix. Real containment needs an authorized incident procedure and fresh evidence; the lab exposes no live-response API.

The original [GuardDuty example](../examples/events/guardduty-credential-access.json) remains an illustrative record for reading practice. It is not generated by the detector or joined to demo events.
